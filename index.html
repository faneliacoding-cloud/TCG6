Founder & CEO

Experience.
Access.
Cultural fluency.

Chris Chambers founded The Chamber Group in 2006 after rising through Mercury Records, EMI, Interscope, and Arista — managing rosters that included Whitney Houston, TLC, Usher, OutKast, Eminem, and P. Diddy. He built this firm on a singular premise: your story deserves to be told at the highest level.

Twenty years of music and entertainment DNA. A network no ordinary agency can replicate. An instinct for cultural timing that is either innate or it isn't.
